Cadence

Cadence is the steady rhythm of play: the rally, the footfalls, the weekly lesson that becomes a habit. A club thrives when its players find a cadence, every Tuesday at 19:00. The app makes that rhythm easy to keep.

How it ties to what we are building

Weekly class templates that repeat on the same day and time.

Eight-session packs and expiry reminders that keep a habit going.

A referral loop that brings a friend into the rhythm.

A rest rule that caps a coach at 6 teaching hours in a row.

Naming proposal

10

Cadence is the warmest and most approachable of the four. It speaks to habit, retention and the weekly rhythm of a club. It is the closest alternative to Kairos if the brand should feel more energetic. Its main risk is how crowded the name is.
