The insight

Nobody books an hour. They book their hour.

Saturday at 18:00 after work. Tuesday at 07:00 before the office. The slot is the same sixty minutes. What it makes possible is not.

Kairos · Naming proposal

04

This is the idea the name carries. A booking platform could be about inventory: courts and time slots. Ours is about the moment a player chooses. Everything in the product, from the court grid to the waitlist, exists to get that person into their hour.
