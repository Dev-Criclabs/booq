The word

Two Greek words for time

The clock

Chronos

Time that is counted. Ten o’clock, sixty minutes, a slot on the wall.

The moment

Kairos

Time that is chosen. The right hour to act, the instant worth seizing.

A court has hours. A player wants the right one.

Kairos · Naming proposal

03

The Greeks had two words for time. Chronos is the clock, time you count. Kairos is the opportune moment, time you choose. Our platform already counts time perfectly: every booking is sixty minutes on the hour. What players actually want is the right hour, and that is kairos.
