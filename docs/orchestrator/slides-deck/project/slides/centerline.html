Centerline

The centre line splits a court into two equal halves. Both sides play on the same footing, by the same lines. That is the promise of a well-run club: a fair system. Centerline is the neutral platform that keeps every booking fair and every credit counted.

Fair play, built in.

Where every booking lines up.

How it ties to what we are building

A court or a coach is never double-booked, even while a payment is pending.

Credits and the wallet are ledgers that always balance.

One set of rules for every club, each club setting its own numbers.

The court grid is the heart of the app.

Watch-out: American spelling (British is Centreline), a longer name, and a term common in engineering. Not yet checked.
