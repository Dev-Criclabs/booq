Charter

A charter is a founding document that grants a place and its rights. The name treats each club as an institution with its own rules and members. It also has a second meaning: to charter a court is to reserve it as your own.

Run your club by its own rules.

Your club, your charter.

How it ties to what we are building

Every club sets its own rules, prices and policies as settings.

Money goes straight to the club, never through the platform.

The platform is the framework behind each club.

Every change to a rule or setting is written to an audit log.

Watch-out: several large companies use the exact word, and it can feel stiff to casual players. Not yet checked.

Charter is the most formal and the most private-club sounding of the four. It flatters the club owner, which matters because owners are the buyers. It speaks to authority and independence, but it may feel serious for casual players.
