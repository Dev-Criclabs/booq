# Gate 3 check: F06, F10 and F13 task lists

Reviewer, 30 Oct 2026. The three task lists were not on `main` yet, so they were read from `origin/spec/tasks-f06-f10-f13` (`specs/features/F06-packages-credit-wallet/tasks.md` 25 tasks counting T06a and T06b, `F10-line-messages/tasks.md` 22, `F13-receipts-reports/tasks.md` 21). Compared with the approved requirements and the draft designs on `origin/main` (head 3d82473), the F03 to F05 task lists, the migration rules of the build plan (`migrate.ts` refuses a duplicate number and refuses a number below one already applied), and `docs/review/checklist.md`. AC coverage was counted by script from the `IDs:` lines with the sweep tasks left out, so a sweep that names a whole feature does not count.

## Verdict

**Not ready to approve.** Three blocking items and eight should-fix items. The task lists are in much better shape than the F04 and F05 drafts: every task names IDs and a role, every task is S or M, and every task has a "Tests first" line (F06-T24 and the other sweeps say "Done when"). The blocks are about gate order and migration numbers.

## Blocking

### G3B-1 | blocking | The designs these tasks come from are not approved
The status line of `F06/design.md`, `F10/design.md` and `F13/design.md` on `main` reads "DRAFT for approval (gate 2) ... The Architect reviews it", and `docs/decisions.md` has no decision approving any of them (D82 approved the F04 and F05 designs; D100 to D128 do not mention F06, F10 or F13 designs). The development plan says gate 3 follows an approved design and "a role whose feature is not approved at gates 1 and 2 does not write code" (checklist 0.1). The requirements are approved (D83 to D99, D118, D119). Fix: a person approves the three designs (or the Orchestrator records a decision that the Architect's review is the approval), then these task lists are checked again. Do not start F06-T01, F10-T02 or F13-T01 before that.

### G3B-2 | blocking | F06 migration numbers do not rise in task order
The header says "rising with task order". Dependencies say otherwise:

`applyMigrations` refuses a migration numbered below one already applied, so T07, T05, T08, T09, T10, T11 and T12 cannot merge in dependency order. Fix: renumber so numbers rise with the order tasks merge (for example T04 0504, T06a 0505, T06b 0506, T07 0507, T05 0508, T08 0509, T09 0510, T10 0511, T11 0512, T12 0513), and update the design's file table.

### G3B-3 | blocking | F13 gives two tasks the same migration number
F13-T08 ("Migration 0706 part one") and F13-T09 ("Migration 0706 part two") both own 0706. A merged migration is never edited and `loadMigrations` throws on a duplicate number, so the second task cannot be written. F13-T10 uses 0705 but depends only on "F07 coach pay fields", so it can land after 0706 and fail the order rule. F13-T11 owns two files (0707 and 0708) although the header says "one task per file". Fix: one file per task (give T09 its own number, for example 0707, and move policies and settings to 0708 and 0709), put T10 before T08 in dependency order or renumber it, and update the design's file table (`0701` to `0708`).

## Should fix

### G3B-4 | Wrong dependency: F06-T14 on F10-T05
F06-T14 enqueues reminders through `app.enqueue_message` and depends on F10-T05. F10-T05 is migration 0604 (`platform.club_line_usage` and config rows). `app.enqueue_message` is created by F10-T06 (migration 0605). Fix: depend on F10-T06.

### G3B-5 | A new ledger reason is waiting for a rule
F06-T08 adds the wallet reason `import_transfer` to both CHECKs, and F06-T02's test accepts it "only after T08 and the PRD row". The reason is not in the hand-off data model or D78 (wallet reasons end at `import`, plus `expire` when wallet expiry is set), and neither decisions D105 nor D13 names it. The tasks are right to wait, but this is a ledger reason the user has not approved (checklist 7.3). **QUESTION** for the Orchestrator and the user: approve the reason, or transfer an imported balance with `import` on both sides. Keep F06-T08 blocked until the PRD and hand-off rows exist.

### G3B-6 | Acceptance criteria no task names
Counted without the sweeps.
- F06: PKG-02-AC5 (Student package stays valid after 18; T07 and T06a test text covers it but name D84 only), PKG-02-AC6 (an adult cannot buy a Student package; T07's test covers it), PKG-02-AC10 (bundle hours work at any time on either court, a solo credit works with any coach in its tier), PKG-03-AC8 and AC9 (credit reserved before expiry, lost on a later cancel; T06a names D85), R1-AC6 (mixed payment cancelled 12 hours or more ahead: credit to the package, money to the wallet with reason `refund`), R1-AC7 (club closure returns full value), R1-AC10 (solo credit covers the coaching line only), R3-AC6 (buying in a Paused club is refused), R3-AC8 (Student product sold by the owner), R3-AC10 (numbers are variables).
- F10: LINE-07-AC7 (bookings and payments never blocked by the cap; F10-T21 tests it), LINE-07-AC9 and AC10 (last known usage, mid month upgrade; F10-T13 names D97 and D99).
Of these, R1-AC6, R1-AC7 and R1-AC10 need F09 and F07 task lists that do not exist yet; F06 has `wallet_credit` and `credit_return` but no task says which caller makes the cancel and closure moves. Fix: put the AC IDs on the tasks that already test them, and add a note that R1-AC6, AC7 and AC10 are closed by F09 and F07 tasks (name them when they exist).
F13 has no AC unnamed, but 53 of its 68 ACs are covered only by a whole-rule mention such as "R2" or "R5". That is acceptable for gate 3 if the sweep (F13-T21) lists each AC with its test, as it says.

### G3B-7 | Tasks that need Content keys depend on them without naming the task
F10-T10 (Flex templates) "Deps ... Content pending keys", F13-T06 (PDF renderer) "Deps T01, Content keys", F06-T17 to T20 (screens) need the F06 keys. The keys are F10-T20, F13-T19 and F06-T21, all listed after the tasks that need them. Fix: name the task numbers in the dependencies and run them first, or state that a task starts with the keys it already has and adds the rest in `locales/pending/`.

### G3B-8 | F10's critical path waits on a parked user action
F10-T01 (checks G1 to G4 against the club's real Official Account) depends on OA test access that "is parked with the user". The tasks that follow it (T08, T09, T10, T11, T12, T13, T15, T16, T19) are 9 of the 22. The ops plan (spec-consistency OPS-2) still puts the real OA on 21 Dec, after the 7 Dec test start. Fix: the Orchestrator sets a date for OA access, or the list says which tasks use the fake LINE until then (the QA fake LINE exists).

### G3B-9 | Dependencies on lists that do not exist yet
F13-T10 depends on "F07 coach pay fields" and F13-T14 and T18 on payouts; F06 refers to F09 return calls; F07, F08 and F09 have designs and requirements but no task lists on `main`. Say so in the status lines as F04-T14 does ("not startable until ...").

### G3B-10 | F13-T04 edits other features' call sites
F13-T04 "wire callers (late, duplicate, wrong amount credit, zero money confirm, cash route, wallet credit orders)" touches code owned by F04 and F05 tasks, which are also BE-C. Confirm it adds a port call through the registered port only and edits no merged F04 or F05 migration (a merged migration is never edited). State the files it may touch.

### G3B-11 | Migration block table is incomplete
F13's design says "the build plan names blocks only up to F06 and F10 took 0601 to 0699 in its draft" (assumption A-1). F07, F08, F09 and F14 have no stated block, so a collision with F13's 0701 to 0799 is possible. Fix: the Architect publishes one table (F01 0001 to 0099, F02 0101 to 0199, F03 0201 to 0299, F04 0301 to 0399, F05 0401 to 0499, F06 0501 to 0599, F10 0601 to 0699, F13 0701 to 0799, F07 onward) in the build plan.
