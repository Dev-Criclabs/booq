## Blocking

### G3C-1 | blocking | F04-T22 and F08-T05 depend on each other
F08-T05 (`take_seat` and the class functions, migration 0905) lists "Deps T03, F07-T03, F03-T30, F04-T22". F04-T22 (`order_line.kind` gains `class_seat`; Pay calls `app.take_seat`) lists "Deps F04-T12 (Pay), F08-T05". Neither can start first. F08-T12 and F08-T05 also need F04-T22 for the class seat line. Fix: split F04-T22 into (a) the migration that adds the `class_seat` line kind and its pricing columns, which F08-T05 needs, and (b) the Pay change that calls `take_seat` and accepts an offer's held booking, which needs F08-T05. Make (a) depend on F04-T12 only, and F08-T05 on (a). F04-T22 as written also bundles three changes (line kind, pricing from credit, wallet or the drop-in price, claim of a held booking), which is more than a day on its own.

### G3C-2 | blocking | Cross-feature migration numbers are not deterministic and sit below the high-water mark
F03-T30 and F03-T31 say "Migration 0225 / 0226 **or the next free `02xx` number**", and F04-T22 says "0314 or the next free `03xx` number". Two problems.
1. "Or the next free number" is not a task decision; the number must be named so the order rule can be checked (checklist 13.7, G3-2).
2. `applyMigrations` refuses any migration numbered below the highest already applied (`if (m.version < highest) throw`, out of order). F03's block (0201 to 0299) and F04's (0301 to 0399) already have neighbours at 0401 and above on `main` (F05) and F06 to F13 migrations are planned above them. A database that has applied a higher migration, such as staging and the production test window from 7 Dec, refuses 0225, 0226 and 0314 when they arrive later. The same applies to F06-T25 (0515) once F07's 0801 is applied. `docs/proposals/migration-blocks.md` itself leaves "whether numbering could follow the build waves" open for the Architect.
Fix (an Architect decision, not the Reviewer's): either the runner is changed to apply a lower-numbered migration that has not been applied yet (checksum still checked, order still deterministic on a fresh database), or late additions take the next number above the global highest. Until then these four tasks cannot be approved. This check also reaches back to F04-T22's predecessors: any late addition in a lower block has the same problem. Four-digit numbers (F09 1001 to 1009) are fine: the loader's file pattern is four digits and equal-length zero-padded strings compare correctly.

## Should fix

### G3C-3 | F07 acceptance criteria R5-AC1 to AC6 are named by no task
F07 R5 ("solo class bookings and the booking record") has six ACs: a solo class stores both coach and court and the database refuses one without the other (AC1), a two hour selection is one order with one solo booking per hour and the rest rule is checked every hour (AC2), future solo hours count toward the limit (AC3, D49), the confirmed card names coach and court and the coach gets New session (AC4), played at the end of the slot and K5 attendance (AC5), a price change after booking keeps the booked price (AC6, D120). None appears in any F07 `IDs:` line. Several are probably exercised by F07-T06 and F07-T07 (claim and Pay) but the tasks do not say so, so the F07-T21 sweep cannot hold them. Fix: name R5-AC1 to AC6 on the tasks that test them (F07-T06 for AC1 and AC2, F07-T10 for AC4, F03 and F04 for AC3 and AC6).

### G3C-4 | F08 CLASS-01-AC18 is a test-data rule
It says the canvas sample sessions are not used in tests (Gaps B1, B2). Name it in F08-T20 or F08-T21 as a rule for fixtures, not as a feature.

Judged by the number of separate functions or behaviours in one migration:

- F09-T06: `cancel_booking` covering locks in the FA 3.4 order, outcome, returns, status, occupancy deletion for court and coach, `restore_line`, promo and partner counts, the `booking_cancel` row, handlers and messages.

Each is labelled S or M. If the author can show each fits a day, say so in the task; otherwise split along the function boundaries (the F05-T05a to c split is the pattern).
